FAQs

Questions, answered clearly.

Is registration free?⌄

Yes. Creating a TrainAI account is free. Be careful of anyone asking you to pay an activation or registration fee. No such fee is required by this landing page.

Can I withdraw the 5,000 UGX starter bonus immediately?⌄

No. It is a locked promotional amount. The stated eligibility requirement is three eligible tasks approved through review. The bonus remains locked until your account confirms that the eligibility conditions have been met.

When are task rewards added to my balance?⌄

After you submit a task, it may be reviewed. Only approved submissions qualify for the reward configured for that task. Submitting work does not guarantee approval or payment.

Can I receive money through MTN MoMo or Airtel Money now?⌄

Mobile money payout processing is not active yet. MTN MoMo and Airtel Money are intended payout options, but no withdrawal should be treated as available until payment processing is configured, tested and funded. Your account balance is not a promise of an immediate payout.

Are tasks and earnings guaranteed every day?⌄

No. Task availability, eligibility and rewards may vary. TrainAI does not guarantee daily tasks, a fixed income or a particular amount of earnings.
